A Letter For You

Chiquita, I've started this letter more times than I can count, because nothing I write feels big enough for how grateful I am to have you. So I'm going to stop trying to make it perfect and just tell you the truth. You make ordinary days feel like something worth remembering. A quiet Tuesday, a bad-traffic afternoon, a night when neither of us had plans — you turn all of it into something I look forward to. I don't think you know how rare that is. I'm grateful for the small things most people never see: the way you check in on me without making it a big deal, the way you laugh at your own jokes before you finish telling them, the way you show up for the people you love without being asked. I notice. I notice all of it. I think about the memories we've already made and I smile without even meaning to — and then I think about everything we haven't done yet, and I get excited all over again. There's a version of our future I keep imagining, and in every version, you're there. I'm proud of you. Not in a small way — in the way where I watch you handle hard things with more grace than you give yourself credit for, and I want to tell everyone I know how lucky I am. So today isn't really about a holiday. It's just an excuse for me to say, out loud, in writing, where you can't scroll past it too fast: thank you for choosing me, for staying, for being exactly who you are. Happy International Girlfriend Day, Chiquita. Love, Always yours.
